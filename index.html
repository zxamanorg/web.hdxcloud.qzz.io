❓ FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Got questions? We've got answers. If you don't find what you're looking for, our Discord team is always available.

Is the hosting really completely free? +

Yes — 100% free, no credit card required, no hidden fees, no paid upgrades. HDX-CLOUD is a community-driven platform offering free Minecraft hosting to everyone. We don't have paid tiers because we believe everyone deserves a free server.

How do I get started? +

Simply click any "Get Started Free" button on this page, which will take you to our Discord server. Once there, verify yourself and open a server request ticket. Our staff will set up your server and provide connection details within minutes.

Do you support both Java and Bedrock editions? +

Yes! HDX-CLOUD supports both Java Edition and Bedrock Edition servers. We also support Geyser/Floodgate setups that allow cross-play between Java and Bedrock players on the same server.

Can I install mods and plugins? +

Absolutely. We support Bukkit, Spigot, Paper, Forge, Fabric, and popular modpacks. You can install plugins and mods via the file manager or request our staff to install them for you through Discord. No restrictions on what you can run.

Is there DDoS protection? +

Yes, all HDX-CLOUD servers include enterprise-grade DDoS protection at no extra cost. Our network filtering absorbs attack traffic before it reaches your server, keeping it online even during active attacks.

Is Discord required to use HDX-CLOUD? +

Yes, Discord is our primary platform for server management, support, and communication. It acts as your onboarding portal and support hub. Discord accounts are free and the setup takes under 30 seconds if you don't already have one.

How fast is the setup process? +

Most servers are set up within under 2 minutes after submitting your request. During busy periods it may take up to 10 minutes. Our average setup time across all users is just 90 seconds from request to live server.

Can I manage my server from mobile? +

Yes! Our control panel is fully mobile-responsive. You can start, stop, restart your server, view console logs, manage files, and check player activity from any smartphone or tablet browser without any issues.
